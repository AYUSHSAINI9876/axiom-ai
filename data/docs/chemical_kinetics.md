# Advanced Chemical Kinetics: Transition State Theory in Complex Molecular Systems

## 1. Introduction
The study of chemical kinetics has evolved from simple collision theory to sophisticated transition state theory (TST). This document explores the non-RRKM (Rice-Ramsperger-Kassel-Marcus) behavior in the thermal decomposition of complex hydrocarbons.

## 2. The Eyring Equation
The rate constant k for a chemical reaction can be described by the Eyring-Polanyi equation:
k = (κ k_B T / h) exp(ΔS‡ / R) exp(-ΔH‡ / RT)
Where κ is the transmission coefficient, k_B is the Boltzmann constant, and ΔH‡ is the enthalpy of activation.
